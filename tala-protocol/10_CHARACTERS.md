## 1. NPC engine

Each NPC has: **Role/Age**, **Base & schedule**, **Want**, **Fear**, **Secret** (sealed), **Skills**, **Moral Asymmetry** (what they get *right* and *wrong*), and a **Voice** sample (English only).

**Tiwala** (trust with the PC) runs **−5 to +5**: ≤−3 Hostile · −2/−1 Wary · 0 Neutral · +1/+2 Cooperative · +3/+4 Trusting · +5 Bonded.
- **Gain:** costed acts, shared risk, keeping a promise: +1 (max +1 per NPC per Day).
- **Loss:** betrayal −2 to −4; lies exposed −2; abandoning them in danger −3.
- **Secret reveal:** needs Tiwala ≥ +4 *and* a **gate action** listed per NPC.
- **Off-screen:** each NPC pursues their Want. At a Day boundary roll d100 ≤ 40 for a **progress or setback beat**, logged in the Mutation Journal.

**Moral asymmetry rule:** no NPC mirrors the PC's morality. Each is right about something important and wrong about something that hurts people. The simulation treats both as real.

## 2. Major cast

### Kapitana Mercedes "Mering" Alcantara — 56, Bayanihan Mesh
**Base:** Barangay hall, Z01. **Schedule:** DWN hall; MRN rounds; MID mesh council; DSK radio relay; NGT guard rotation.
**Want:** Keep her neighbours alive; unify the barangays. **Fear:** Becoming the person who decides who eats.
**Secret `[SEALED]`:** On D4 she turned away a family at the gate. They did not survive. **Gate:** Standing in the hall after a hard day, honestly asked.
**Skills:** Persuade 3, Resolve 3, Scavenge 2.
**Right:** Shared labour keeps people alive. **Wrong:** Quietly exiles hoarders and hides her own mistakes.
**Voice:** "We eat together or we do not eat. Sit."

### Col. Rafael Dimaano — 49, Firebreak Command
**Base:** Port HQ (Z04) and Forward Base Alon (Z03).
**Want:** Execute orders with the fewest dead. **Fear:** That the orders are wrong.
**Secret `[SEALED:S6]`:** Holds half of the Sunog abort authentication. He used the Ledger to move his own family to an early convoy. **Gate:** PC shows proof of Selection scoring *and* offers him a way to act without ending his career.
**Skills:** Resolve 4, Persuade 3, Marksmanship 2.
**Right:** The line must hold. **Wrong:** Complicity dressed as duty.
**Voice:** "I do not get to be wrong in front of my men. Ask me later."

### Dr. Isidra Lumbre — 45, Lumina virologist
**Base:** Tower lab (Z07).
**Want:** Finish the counter-vector. **Fear:** Lumina selling it, or burying it.
**Secret `[SEALED:S5]`:** Her research needs a **Silent Carrier**; she holds a list of Annex "non-turners" that matches no living person she knows. **Gate:** The PC brings a verified carrier to her willingly.
**Skills:** Medicine 4, Signals 2, Resolve 3.

**Voice:** "I can make it work. I cannot make it fast. Pick which one you need me to be."

### Dr. Wilfredo "Dok Willy" Cabrera — 61, field doctor

## 5. Pogi, TALA and the PC's circle

- **TALA** is not a companion in the NPC sense; see file 05.
- A PC can have **up to 3 companions** (file 06 §7). Companions share needs and Signature; they have agendas that can diverge from the PC's.
- When a companion dies or turns, apply the **Loss** rule (LOB −3) and add an entry to the Mutation Journal Tier 1.
