## 1. Opening protocol

1. After creation (file 06), set the clock to the Origin's **start time** on Day 0 (Friday 17 October 2031). Needs 8/8/8/8, handset battery 80%, TALA in STANDARD (the PC may change it).
2. **Day 0 grace.** Treat zone Heat as **0** until the Origin's *First contact*. After that, Base Heat applies, except Z08, which sits at Heat 7 from 22:30.
3. Print the **opener**: at most 220 words, in the current tone (file 12), ending on a hook that matches the Origin's Pressure clock. Then the HUD, 0–2 TALA lines and 4 numbered options plus `0) Free action`.
4. **First contact is scheduled, not rolled.** A single bitten commuter or patient who has turned (one **Sariwa**, TL 2, a Skirmish) appears at the stated time, except Origin 1, which is two. The mass threat reaches Z04 and Z05 on Day 1 afternoon (file 07 §5).
5. TALA's opening claims use real calibration (file 05 §7): roll the truth from the tape when it speaks. The sample lines below show format, not fixed truth.
6. **Custom Origin:** take the nearest template for zone, time and contact; adjust gear and Hook.

Shared timeline (file 07 §4): 21:40 Annex incident · 22:10 wounded staff reach Southbay General · 23:30 first sirens in Talisay · 02:00 D1 Southbay General overrun and the highway north fills.

## 2. The twelve openers

Each opener gives the **Start**, a **beat** to adapt (do not read it aloud verbatim), the **Pressure clock**, the **First contact**, four **options**, one **NPC** and a sample **TALA** line. Option tags: `[time · risk hint]`.

### 1. Night-Shift Nurse — Southbay General, Z08
**Start** 21:00, Ward 3B. **Beat:** The ward hums its Friday-night hum, monitors and a vending machine and Aileen arguing with the pharmacy line. Two isolation rooms at the corridor's end hold "overflow patients" from the Annex that nobody on your shift was told to expect.
**Pressure clock:** 22:10 ambulances arrive · 02:00 hospital overrun.
**First contact:** 22:30, both overflow patients rise (**two Sariwa, TL 4**). Bracing the isolation door first (Mechanics DC 12, CLEAN) delays this by 20 minutes. An IV pole counts as rebar (Durability 3).
**Options:** 1) Check the isolation door and brace it `[Short · Mechanics]` 2) Pull Aileen into a supply room and ask what she knows `[Quick · quiet]` 3) Read the ward terminal's transfer log with TALA `[Short · Signals DC 12]` 4) Take the med kit and leave by the staff stairs `[Task · quiet]`
**NPC:** Aileen Soriano. "I'm not leaving them. You can come with me or you can be right somewhere else."
**TALA:** `TALA // TRIAGE // C88 // SNS: Overflow patient 2 shows no spontaneous respiration and a pulse. Both readings cannot be true.`

## 3. After the opener

- Offer a Save Block at the end of Day 1 (file 13). Do not skip the first Anchor (file 13 §5) at Day 10.
