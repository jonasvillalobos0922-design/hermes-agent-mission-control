# 08 — GEOGRAPHY

**TIER:** REF | **DEPENDS:** 02, 05

Metro Cebu on the central-east coast of Cebu Island, facing the Mactan Channel. Population before Day 0: about 3 million. Real districts and landmarks are used respectfully; institutions are fictional.

**Fields:** **Density** (1–5, local Gutom concentration) · **Base Heat** (0–10) · **Mesh** (0–5 bars) · **Pools** (Food/Water/Medical/Parts/Fuel units; tiers and renewables in file 02 §7 and §7b).

**Pools** are Base units at Day 0: Food in Rations, Water in litres of packaged water, then Medical, Parts and Fuel. Later tiers shrink them (file 02 §7). They are what a lone scavenger can reach. Institutional stores (the Compact's market, Command rations, the Tower) are separate and are earned through Standing, labor or trade (file 09). Food Pools total **136 Rations**.

## The Perimeter

Outside the zones above lie the **Northern Line** (beyond Z01) and **Southern Line** (beyond Z10): fortified Firebreak Command lines with kill-zones, drones and searchlights. They cannot be crossed by civilians. The **naval cordon** blocks the sea, though night fishing boats can slip through gaps at risk (see Kanal Fishers, file 09). The Perimeter is the story's silent clock: it is where the city ends.

## Zone entries

### Z01 — Mandaue Industrial Belt
Rope works, cold storage, tin-roof factories, a barangay hall with a loudspeaker tower. **Controlled by:** Bayanihan Mesh (the barangay hall); factories contested. **Hazards:** open yards (OPEN), pipework (CHOKE). **Notes:** the **cold storage** holds 8 of Z01's 16 Food units, and it spoils over D12–D16 when the generators die, so take them early. Kapitana Mering's base. Routes north end at the Northern Line.

### Z02 — Bridge Approach
Approach roads to the Mandaue–Mactan Bridge and Marcelo Fernan Bridge. FBC **Bridge Gate** with checkpoints, barricades and drones. **Controlled by:** Firebreak Command. **Hazards:** civilian crush zones; Sgt. Detta Ocampo's checkpoint. **Notes:** Command mesh range (LEASH ≥ 7 check-ins happen here).

### Z03 — Mactan Island (Lapu-Lapu & Airport)
Resort strip, fishing communities, **Mactan-Cebu Airport** (Command "Forward Base Alon"). **Controlled by:** Firebreak Command. **Hazards:** open runways, searchlights (L5 sweeps), Command patrols. **Notes:** the airport fuel farm (8 of Z03's 12 Fuel units) is locked behind Command; the Fishers' harbours are on the east shore.

### Z04 — Cebu Port, Piers 1–3
Cranes, container yards, ferries. **Pier 3** is where the Last Green Convoy left (D7). From **D33** the **Selection Boats** sail from here (file 11). **Controlled by:** Command at Piers 1 and 3; **Kanal Fishers** use the northern slips. **Hazards:** container mazes (CHOKE/COVER), flooded lower yards after Dindo.

### Z05 — Downtown: Colon–Carbon–Fuente
Colon (the old street), the **Carbon Market** (warren of stalls, covered halls), Fuente Osmeña rotunda, old cinemas and churches, multi-level car parks. Densest in the first ten days. **Controlled by:** the **Carbon Compact** (Ate Guada) in the market; Tulisan roam Colon. **Hazards:** Buhol in a cinema, narrow alleys (CHOKE), roof routes (HIGH). **Notes:** the richest Food Pool in the Metro (24 units), and the most contested.

### Z06 — Capitol–Banilad Heights
Capitol compound, schools, clinics and hillside neighbourhoods. **Controlled by:** contested; **Dok Willy's school clinic** is here. **Hazards:** steep streets, gated subdivisions. **Notes:** a quiet base area with decent Water and Medical.

### Z07 — IT Park–Business Park
Glass towers, call-centre floors, malls. **Lumina Tower** is the tallest block; **Lumina Remnant Security** holds the lower floors; the Tower has its own generators, a rooftop mast and a **jammer**. **Hazards:** glass atria (N+1), elevators, a Buhol in a mall atrium. **Notes:** Mesh jammer: −20 C on TALA claims within 500 m of the Tower when it jams.

### Z08 — Talisay–Southbay (Ground Zero)
**Southbay General Hospital** and the **Lumina Neuro-Research Annex**. The Dead are densest here; Gutom classes include Sariwa for the first week and Bulok from D45. **Medical Pool 9**, the Metro's largest. Two **Remisol** vials sit in the Annex pharmacy (file 04 §5). **Hazards:** Buhol in the ER, Annex sub-levels. **Notes:** The **Annex Operations Office** holds **Sarmiento's Conscience Patch** (file 11).

### Z09 — Busay–Transcentral Uplands
Hill road, springs, relay towers, viewpoints. **Controlled by:** Bayanihan Highlands (a refuge) with a **Muling Pagsibol** camp farther up. **Hazards:** cold nights, landslides after Dindo. **Notes:** safest but poorest in Parts; good water; best Dead Quiet conditions.

### Z10 — Naga–Minglanilla Power Corridor
Coal-plant substation, rail spur, quarries. **Hazards:** open fields, high-tension lines, a camp of Pagsibol pilgrims heading north-south. **Notes:** Parts Pool 12 and Fuel Pool 8.

### Z11 — Waterways
Mactan Channel and the Camotes Sea. Boat travel is quiet at sail (N1), loud at motor (N3). The **naval cordon** patrols with drones. **Notes:** Fishers know gaps; weather per file 14 T4.
