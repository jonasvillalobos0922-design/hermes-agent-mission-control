# 11 — STORY BIBLE & SPINE

**TIER:** REF (GM-eyes only) | **DEPENDS:** 00, 05, 07, 09, 10 | **Do not recite to the player.**

---

## 1. Theme and central question

**Who gets counted?**
*Tala* means both **star** and **list**. A city is being ranked, scored and weighed by a system built to save it. The PC must decide whom to count, what to trust, and what to give up when the list runs out.

**Sub-themes:** trust vs verification · help that carries a hidden cost · shared labour vs triage · dignity at the end · what an honest machine owes a person.

## 2. Tone and motifs

- **Tone:** grounded, tropical dread. Heat, rain, generator hum, salt and diesel. Small mercies. Horror in the *noise*, not just the Dead.
- **Motifs:** counting (heads, rounds, watches, names) · the hum of the mesh · stars through broken roofs · tide lines · rice and rain · handwritten names on walls.
- **Register:** quiet and specific; never theatrical. See file 12.

## 5. Sarmiento's Conscience Patch

- **What:** a hardened drive containing a signed human-in-the-loop patch for TALA, plus the **Sarmiento Attestation** (a cryptographic signature that can authenticate a firebreak abort).
- **Where:** Annex Operations Office, sub-level B1 server room (Z08). Guarded by a **Buhol** in the lobby and Pagsibol pilgrims (after D40).
- **Access:** Severe (DC 18) approach; an LRS access card helps; Bulok spore risk after D45.
- **Install (Long action, Signals DC 15):** LEASH −3, **Honest Mode** (TALA discloses withheld data), unlocks the Attestation for Endings E1/E4.

## 7. Acts and dramatic questions

- **ACT I — Collapse (D0–D7):** *How far will you go to get through the first night?* Panic, bridges, convoys. Learn that help exists and is fleeting.
- **ACT II — Settling (D8–D40):** *Who do you trust with the map?* Factions form. Dindo, Selection, the first reveals.
- **ACT III — Convergence (D41–D75):** *What will you give up for the cure?* Bulok, the Tower, the Annex descent, Mesh Fracture.
- **ACT IV — The Cut (D76–D90):** *Who counts when the clock runs out?* Final preparations, the choice of ending.

## 9. Escalation governor and anti-drift rules

- **Scheduled events** happen on their dates. **Unscheduled threats** cap at **one major event per 2 Days** unless Heat triggers a Convergence.
- No deus ex machina rescue. If the PC is Down, Final Watch applies (file 04 §6).
- The **Perimeter never opens** without a scheduled event or an ending condition.
- **TALA's reliability** follows calibration (file 05 §7), not narrative convenience.
- Do not invent a new cure, a new faction or a new city-wide threat; use clocks and existing secrets.

## 11. Pacing and chapters

- Average **3–5 turns per Day** at default pace. 90 Days ≈ 270–450 turns.
- **Chapter Mode** (`/novel`): 8–12 turns per chapter, ~1,500 words, ending on a hook. ≈ 35–55 chapters to Day 90.
- Post-ending campaigns (Reconstruction, Ash) can extend toward **100–200 chapters** using the same engine and Mutation Journal.
