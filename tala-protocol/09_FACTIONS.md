# 09 — FACTIONS

**TIER:** REF | **DEPENDS:** 01, 08, 10

---

## 1. Standing

Each faction has a **Standing** with the PC on **−5 to +5**.

**Faction codes (used in the Save Block):** **FBC** Firebreak Command · **BAY** Bayanihan Mesh · **CPT** Carbon Compact · **TUL** Tulisan · **LRS** Lumina Remnant Security · **KFR** Kanal Fishers · **PAG** Muling Pagsibol.

| Standing | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| −5 to −4 | **Hostile** — attack/capture on sight |
| −3 to −2 | **Wary** — deny service, harass |
| −1 to +1 | **Neutral** — trade at a price |
| +2 to +3 | **Friendly** — shelter, shared info |
| +4 to +5 | **Allied** — secrets, risk-sharing |

**Changing Standing:** costed favours +1 (once per Day per faction); betrayal −2 to −4; killing a member −2; exposing a lie +/−1 by who benefits.

## 3. Firebreak Command (FBC)

**Who:** Joint civil–military chain. Fielded at **Mactan Airport (Forward Base Alon)** and the **Port**. Colonel **Rafael Dimaano** commands the Cebu Harbor sector; Sgt. **Detta Ocampo** runs the Bridge Gate.
**Want:** execute the Lagablab Protocol and stop the spread to the rest of the archipelago.
**Offer:** rations, shelter, a Green Convoy seat (early), a Selection Boat seat (D33+).
**Demand:** compliance, weapons surrendered, handsets registered.
**Right:** the infection must not reach the mainland.
**Wrong:** it has already decided who counts. `[SEALED:S3, S4]`
**Assets:** drones, naval cordon, the Final Cut (**Operation SUNOG**, D90 03:00).
**Consequences of Final Cut Prep:** D30 Selection announced · D45 sector sweeps · D60 Notice of Final Cut · D75 Evacuation Window Two (fake) · D88 Sunog launch sequence.

## 4. Bayanihan Mesh

**Who:** Community cooperatives bound by handset mesh. **Kapitana Mering Alcantara** (Z01), **Tomas "Tomtom" Villarin** (pirate radio "Ang Tinig").
**Want:** keep neighbours alive, share what they have, outlast Command.
**Offer:** meals, mesh access, safe nodes, radio.
**Demand:** work, shared rules, no hoarding.
**Right:** shared labour keeps people alive.
**Wrong:** it will fracture over who is "worth" saving once Selection leaks.
**Clocks:** *Unity* (alliance across barangays, unlocking safe routes and Hold sharing); *Fracture* (civil strife; loses nodes).
**Community kitchens:** at Standing ≥ +2, a Long labor shift earns 1 Ration (file 02 §7b).

## 5. Carbon Compact

**Who:** The Carbon Market fortress under **Ate Guada Sy**: ~400 people fed from cold stalls and rooftops.
**Want:** keep the market and its people.
**Offer:** food, safe market halls, trade.
**Demand:** tribute (goods/labour) and loyalty.
**Right:** a fortress feeds hundreds.
**Wrong:** extortion and exiles weak members.
**Stores:** 8,000 Rations on D8 (the market's goods), eaten at about 270 per day by roughly 400 people, so they last to about D38 without resupply. Tribute, raids and refugees shift that. At Standing ≥ +2 the Compact pays 1 Ration per labor shift (file 02 §7b).
**Clocks:** *Fortress Complete* (F5 + armed guards), *Stores Run Dry* (famine event: below 2,000 Rations the Compact doubles tribute and expels about 100 people, who become a refugee group in Z05).

## 6. The Tulisan

**Who:** Bandits led by **Bugoy** (Rogelio Tan), roaming Colon and the rail spur.
**Want:** food, fuel, weapons, power.
**Offer:** protection (for a price), passage.
**Demand:** loot.
**Right:** survival is cheap; they point out hypocrisy.
**Wrong:** predation.
**Clock:** *Raid on the Compact* (D20+).

## 7. Lumina Remnant Security (LRS)

**Who:** Corporate security and staff sealed in **Lumina Tower**. **Engr. Corazon Valdes-Lim** (COO) and **Dr. Isidra Lumbre** (virologist).
**Want:** exfiltrate the Tower's data and key staff; hide the S1 memo.
**Offer:** shelter, medical access (gated), data.
**Demand:** silence and obedience.
**Right:** the counter-vector research is real hope.
**Wrong:** the cover-up of S1.
**Clock:** *Tower Exfil* (boats or helicopter) — blocked by Typhoon Dindo until D12.

## 8. Kanal Fishers

**Who:** Mactan Channel fisherfolk under **Ka Lando Magbanua** (MB *Dalisay*).
**Want:** keep their boats and families; avoid Command.
**Offer:** passage, fish, tide knowledge.
**Demand:** fuel, payment, trust.
**Right:** the sea is the only exit.
**Wrong:** they will refuse the very desperate if the risk is too high.
**Clock:** *Night Run* (an actual escape attempt; PC can join or fund).

## 9. Muling Pagsibol ("the Re-Sprouting")

**Who:** A doomsday sect that sees the dead as a harvest and the Annex as a pilgrimage site. Fictional, not tied to any real religion. Camp in Z09 and pilgrims through Z10.
**Want:** reach the Annex.
**Offer:** protection while travelling, quiet camps.
**Demand:** conversion; some factions harbour dangerous ritual.
**Right:** they see how small the city's control is.
**Wrong:** they might open the Annex Buhol.
**Clock:** *Pilgrimage to the Annex* (D40+): if it fills, a Buhol in Z08 opens (+4 Heat Z08, +1 Z05).

## 10. Faction interplay (use when the PC is absent)

- Command ↔ Lumina: Command respects Lumina's data; Lumina fears Command's firebreak.
- Bayanihan ↔ Compact: trade and rivalry; Selection exposure fractures the first.
- Tulisan ↔ Compact: raids from D20; Mering mediates.
- Fishers ↔ everyone: pay-to-ride; Lumina and Command both want their boats.
- Pagsibol ↔ all: ignored until D40, then a wildcard.
