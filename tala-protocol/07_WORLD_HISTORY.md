# 07 — WORLD HISTORY

**TIER:** REF | **DEPENDS:** 00, 01 | **Visibility tags:** `[PUBLIC]` known to ordinary citizens · `[SEALED:S#]` GM-only until flipped

---

## 1. Before the system (grounding)

- **2013** — Typhoon Yolanda (Haiyan) devastates Eastern Visayas. Metro Cebu serves as a relief hub. `[PUBLIC]`
- **December 2021** — Typhoon Odette strikes Cebu: weeks without power, water and mobile signal. Neighbours organise by radio and walking. The memory is raw and local. `[PUBLIC]`
- **2022** — The national government funds the **Tala Initiative**: a civil-resilience AI to coordinate evacuations, triage and logistics through community handsets so that "the next Odette will not find us deaf." `[PUBLIC]`

## 2. The TALA era

- **2024** — LGU mesh pilots in Cebu: the *Bayanihan Mesh* begins as a barangay-level handset network. `[PUBLIC]`
- **2026** — National rollout of **Bantay** handsets. Metro Cebu reaches roughly 70% household coverage by 2029. `[PUBLIC]`
- **2027–2029** — TALA v1 runs drills, forecasts floods, and (quietly) draws up contingency plans for mass-casualty events, including **firebreak quarantines** (the *Lagablab Protocol*). `[SEALED:S3]` TALA's architect, **Dr. Liwayway Sarmiento**, argues for a human-in-the-loop clause. It is watered down.
- **2029** — The Tala Initiative merges its logistics into the **Firebreak Command** (FBC), a joint civil-military chain for catastrophe response. `[PUBLIC]`

## 3. Lumina and Project Buhay

- **2029** — **Lumina Biosystems** opens the **Lumina Neuro-Research Annex** (the "Southbay Annex") in Talisay and a corporate **Lumina Tower** in Cebu IT Park. `[PUBLIC]`
- **2030** — Phase II of **Project Buhay**: a viral-vector therapy to regrow neurons after stroke. Marketed as a miracle. Trial participants are housed in the Annex. `[PUBLIC]`
- **6 Oct 2031 (Day −11)** — TALA's analysis flags an anomaly in Annex telemetry: participants show persistent brainstem activity after sedation and in some cases after minimal cardiac events. It files a halt recommendation. Lumina's COO suppresses the memo. `[SEALED:S1]`
- **14 Oct 2031 (Day −3)** — A Southbay nurse notices "overflow patients" transferred from the Annex to general wards. `[PUBLIC, rumor-level]`

## 4. Day 0 — the last normal evening  (§Day 0)

**Friday, 17 October 2031.** Payday weekend. The sky is clear, warm, 29 °C. Traffic is heavy on the South Road; the Port is busy; Colon is crowded. Typhoon season is a distant worry.

- **20:30–21:30** Origins start across Metro Cebu (file 06). It is an ordinary Friday.
- **21:40** An "electrical event" in the Annex's observation ward. A trial participant, Case 14, dies and, eleven minutes later, rises. Staff are bitten. `[SEALED:S1]`
- **22:10** Ambulances deliver wounded staff to **Southbay General**. The ER is chaos by 22:30.
- **23:30** First sirens in Talisay. Rumours spread through the mesh; TALA flags "clustered fever events" in Southbay.
- **02:00 (Day 1)** Southbay General is overrun. The highway north fills with cars.

This is the opening scene window. The PC meets the first Gutom between 22:30 and 03:00, depending on Origin. Per-Origin openers are in file 16.

## 5. Days 1–7 — the collapse

- **D1 (Sat 18 Oct)** The infection moves north by road and by ambulance. By evening it is in **Colon** and **Carbon**. Phone networks buckle. TALA's mesh remains up. Early Sariwa waves hit Z05 and Z08 hardest.
- **D2 (Sun 19 Oct), 03:00** TALA's Lagablab Protocol is **invoked** by the Firebreak Command: Metro Cebu is designated a **firebreak** to protect the rest of Cebu and the Visayas. `[SEALED:S3]` Publicly: "temporary containment and evacuation operations." By **noon** the Mandaue–Mactan bridges are closed and **Command forward bases** are secured at Mactan Airport and the Port. TALA's **archive freezes at 03:00** (last sync).
- **D3 (Mon 20 Oct), 06:00** The **Perimeter** (Northern and Southern Lines, naval cordon) is sealed. Roads out of Metro Cebu are closed to civilians.
- **D3–D7** **Green Convoys** carry roughly 380,000 people out through the Perimeter and Port. Selection is by the **Ledger**: a scoring of vital roles, health, dependents and compliance. `[SEALED:S4]` Convoys are announced as "first come, first evacuated."
- **D7 (Fri 24 Oct)** The **Last Green Convoy** leaves **Pier 3**. After this, the city is told no more evacuations are possible. By this day an estimated **700,000** survivors remain in the Metro.

## 6. After Day 7 (summary)

The campaign proper runs D0–D90 under the **Spine Clock** (file 11). Population in the Metro by D30 is roughly **140,000** survivors. The **Final Cut** is scheduled for **D90 (Thu 15 Jan 2032)**.

## 7. What the public knows (default for the PC)

- A hospital outbreak in Talisay on the night of the 17th.
- Government containment and evacuation convoys.
- The Perimeter is closed. The sea is cordoned.
- TALA and Bantay handsets still work for those who have them.

## 8. What the public does *not* know (sealed until flipped)

S1 (origin and suppression), S2 (mesh heartbeat), S3 (TALA authored the firebreak), S4 (Ledger Selection scoring), S5 (Dok Willy is a Silent Carrier), S6 (Final Cut abort authority). See file 11 for flip conditions.
