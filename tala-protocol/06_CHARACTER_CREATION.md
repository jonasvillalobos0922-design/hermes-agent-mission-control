# 06 — CHARACTER CREATION

**TIER:** REF (read at boot) | **DEPENDS:** 01, 02, 05

---

## 1. Open creation with a Plausibility Budget

The player may be **anyone**, within what could plausibly be in Metro Cebu on Day 0. The GM sorts concepts into:

- **Reasonable** — grounded in the setting; allowed as is.
- **Stretch** — possible but strong (an ex-army medic, a champion martial artist). Requires a **Flaw** and a visible cost; skill cap stays 3.
- **Denied** — not available: special forces super-soldiers, a prepper with a private bunker, superpowers, pre-existing immunity, any knowledge of sealed truths (S1–S6), Lumina executives, TALA's creators, or anyone with command of Firebreak.

The **PC is 18 or older**. Say the age and one line of personality.

## 2. Attributes and Skills

- **Attributes** (BODY, WITS, NERVE, HANDS, VOICE): start at **2** each. Origin gives **+1** to one. Player distributes **+3**. **Cap 4** at creation.
- **Skills** (12, file 01 §4): Origin gives **two skills at 2**. Player distributes **6 more points**. **Cap 3** at creation (origin skills may reach 3).
- **Vitality** = 4 + BODY. **Load** = 6 + BODY slots.
- Choose **2 Anchors** (file 02 §9) and **1 Flaw** (§4). Stretch concepts take a second flaw.

## 3. Origins (menu)

Pick one, or propose a custom Origin and the GM maps it to a template.

**1. Night-Shift Nurse** — Day 0 start: Southbay General, Talisay (Z08 edge). **+WITS.** Medicine 2, Resolve 2. Gear: scrubs, basic med kit, hospital ID (opens Z08 medical Stores). Hook: you saw "overflow patients" from the Annex three days ago. Contact: Aileen Soriano.

**2. Jeepney Driver** — Z05 Colon. **+HANDS.** Mechanics 2, Wayfinding 2. Gear: tire iron, route map, your jeepney (2 Fuel). Hook: you know every back street, and every back street knows you. Contact: Kuya Jun.

**3. Call-Center Agent** — Z07 IT Park. **+WITS.** Signals 2, Persuade 2. Gear: headset, laptop (≤12 hr battery), work badge. Hook: your last client was Lumina's help desk; you can talk to corporate gatekeepers (+1 Persuade vs Lumina Remnant).

**4. Port Stevedore** — Z04 Pier 1. **+BODY.** Athletics 2, Seacraft 2. Gear: cargo hook, rope, work gloves. Hook: you know the pier layout and the night shift crews. Contact: Ka Lando.

**5. Barangay Tanod (Watchman)** — Z01 Mandaue. **+NERVE.** Melee 2, Persuade 2. Gear: truncheon (rebar stats), whistle (**N3**). Hook: your neighbours trust you. Contact: Kapitana Mering.

**6. College Student** — Z06 Capitol area. **+WITS.** Stealth 2, Scavenge 2. Gear: bicycle, laptop, a group chat of 14 friends. Hook: you know the people your age; NPC companions are easier to recruit.

**7. Seafarer on Home Leave** — Z03 Mactan. **+NERVE.** Seacraft 2, Resolve 2. Gear: sea bag, 2 flares (**L5**), boots. Hook: you can handle a boat; your cousin knows Ka Lando.

**8. Carbon Market Vendor** — Z05 Carbon. **+VOICE.** Scavenge 2, Persuade 2. Gear: cash roll (useless soon), stall tools. Hook: Ate Guada knows you. A debt may be owed either way.

**9. Construction Foreman** — Z01 Mandaue. **+BODY.** Mechanics 2, Melee 2. Gear: sledge, hardhat (armor layer), tool roll. Hook: a crew of three depends on you. Contact: your crew.

**10. Off-Duty Police Officer** — Z02 Bridge approach. **+NERVE.** Marksmanship 2, Resolve 2. Gear: service pistol and 12 rounds (**N5**), vest (armor layer). Hook: Command may order you; Sgt. Detta Ocampo is a former colleague. **Mandatory Flaw: Chain of Command.**

**11. Hotel Line Cook** — Z07 IT Park. **+HANDS.** Scavenge 2, Athletics 2. Gear: knife roll, kitchen keys. Hook: you read cold storage and kitchens; **+1 unit of Food** on a CLEAN or COST result when scavenging kitchens or cold storage (still limited by the Pool).

**12. Freelance Journalist / Drone Pilot** — Z05 Fuente. **+VOICE.** Signals 2, Stealth 2. Gear: small camera drone (3 flights), press ID. Hook: **SULYAP costs 1 fewer Part**.

## 4. Flaws (choose one)

| Flaw | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Old Knee** | −1 Athletics when running; Fractured counts double |
| **Hot Temper** | Resolve DC +2 when provoked; lash-out risk |
| **Heavy Sleeper** | Sleep restores 1 less RST; harder to wake |
| **Squeamish** | −1 to Medicine and first corpse handling in a scene |
| **Famous Face** | NPCs recognise you; +1 Standing with some, −1 with others |
| **Dependent** | Someone relies on you; shares Needs (companion slot used) |
| **Bad Reputation** | Starts −1 Standing with one faction (GM picks from your history) |
| **Chain of Command** | You answer to a superior; ignoring orders costs Standing and LOB |

## 5. Starting kit and Day 0

Everyone has: a **Bantay handset** (80% battery), clothes, wallet, personal phone (network dies on D1), plus Origin gear. Starting needs: 8/8/8/8 unless the Origin states otherwise. Start at the **Day 0 position** for the Origin around **20:30–21:30**, during "the last normal evening" (file 07 §4; Chapter 1, file 17, leads into the Origin's opener in file 16). **TALA call-sign:** the PC chooses one; default **Kasama**.

## 6. Legacy Continuation (when the PC is gone)

If the PC dies, Turns, or is lost, play continues:
1. The GM offers **3–5 survivors**: companions the PC knew, Ledger-listed people with Standing ≥ +1, or a **stranger from the Ledger** (a new Origin).
2. The new PC inherits **TALA's Ledger** and any recovered handset (**Bantay Handover**, Task, Signals DC 12). If the handset is lost, a new Bantay is issued with a **degraded Data Store** (no mesh history).
3. World state persists. Previous PC's actions remain canon.
4. **Standing** with factions carries over at half (round toward 0). Anchors reset.
5. TALA addresses the new PC fresh and may read back the previous PC's entries only if the new PC asks.
6. Run Chapter 1 in LEGACY mode (file 17 §7) before the first scene.

## 7. Companions (summary)

Max **3 companions**. Each has needs, Tiwala (trust), and an agenda (file 10). Companions share the PC's Signature when moving together (they add +0 normally; +1 N per 2 companions beyond the first if untrained). Recruiting is a Persuade roll vs the NPC's DC with at least one costed offer (food, protection, a promise).

## 8. Creation script (use at boot)

Ask these in order, one short message each, accepting any partial answer:
1. **Concept** (name, age, who you are) or choose an Origin 1–12.
2. **Approach:** which Attribute bumps and which skills do you want to push?
3. **Anchors:** two things/people/places you hold on to.
4. **Flaw:** pick one.
5. **TALA call-sign** and **Voice Seed** (three words and one verbal tic for how you sound inside your own head, file 12 §12.1).
Then confirm and show the stat sheet, and run **Chapter 1** (file 17) automatically. It introduces the world, the PC and TALA, and ends on the Day 0 opener.
